## Timeout causes in the runner

R23 recorded **21 timeouts in 50 attempts**. Clean contributed **11 draft and two review** timeouts across 22 attempts. Durability repeats contributed **six draft and two review** timeouts across 28 attempts. R22 clean had 11 draft and nine review timeouts across 28 attempts. R23's four terminal draft-timeout rows were GS-018, GS-023, GS-030 and GS-140. All 21 timeout attempts returned a synthetic Core handoff within the authorized turn, which the harness classified as an agent error. [Trace timings and retry rules](kb-rerun23-diagnosis-evidence.md#attempt-traces)

- The initial R23 split reserved 12 s for review and 3 s for delivery. At the scheduled revision, `1cbf6e4` had changed the minimum review reserve to **9 s**, with **3 s** for delivery and up to **24 s** for an early review. The runner takes the earlier of the capsule expiry and the 60 s answer deadline per attempt. Capsule expiry is rounded to whole seconds. Prefetch gets at most 6 s before the shared drafting cutoff and Core reads run three at a time with deduplicated article windows and spans. In the traces, draft timeouts returned at roughly 48 s, and the four review timeouts returned by 57 s. Two review calls hit the separate 24 s cap with roughly 20 s of nominal turn time left. The traces preserve the cutoffs and timely fallback, while exact capsule expiry and delivery slack per row are unavailable. - The runner awaits one non-streaming `agent.generate` at `deadline − 3 s − 9 s`. Review starts after a completed draft. `runUntil` aborts a slow call and returns the typed stage timeout. The 10-step cap cannot interrupt reasoning inside one inference call. The 3,072-token cap bounds output volume, including reasoning, and the provider still controls when a response completes. The staged budget protects authorization and fallback. Answer completion requires both sequential calls to finish inside their windows.
