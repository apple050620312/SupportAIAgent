## GS-018 and the 60-second budget

**The most likely cause of the persistent GS-018 failure is incomplete draft procedure coverage.** The draft treats recommending SFTP and explaining login as sufficient for a transfer test. It leaves out the explicit instruction to upload the original JAR in the SFTP client after connecting. The prompt singles out the remote-pane action for folder transfers. That asymmetry is a plausible contributor. The cited SFTP passage already supports uploading a file after login, and all ten attempts admitted it alongside the panel guide and mod fallback. All seven returned drafts stopped short of the explicit action, including the three whose reviews timed out. The four completed reviews accepted claim support and rejected completeness on that same action. Each rejection led to a Core handoff. All four post-handoff draft diagnostics were correct under the historical rubric. [GS-018 attempt ledger](kb-rerun25-diagnosis-evidence.md#gs-018-every-attempt)

The direct cause of the **clean unscored result** was deadline exhaustion. Attempts one and two reached the draft cutoff without text. On attempt three a 42.804 s draft left 13.807 s for review, which was cut off without a verdict. The harness recorded `agent-error` and made no judge call. R24 reached a verdict on retry and had five scored handoffs across the cohort for unsupported attribution or wrong compatibility order. R25 fixed the panel article admission gap and the four completed reviews moved to a specific omission. The traces measure client elapsed time. Provider-side queueing, inference time and the effective upstream route remain unknown. [Deadline and retry evidence](kb-rerun25-diagnosis-evidence.md#timeout-ledger)
