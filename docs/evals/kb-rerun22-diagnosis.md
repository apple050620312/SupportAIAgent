## Timeout causes in the runner

At the evaluated revision, the turn implementation is `createVercelTicketRunner().run` in `packages/agent-loop/src/index.ts`. There is no `turn.ts` in that tree. The runner starts a **60 s deadline per attempt**, after initial authentication and before opening history. The eval harness starts each retry with a fresh ticket context, history directory and timer. Three full timeouts plus 1 s and 2 s backoffs account for about 183 s per unscored item. The final semantic judge and the handoff draft judge run after the answer attempt and use a separate request budget. [Runner](../../packages/agent-loop/src/index.ts#L270) · [Eval retry and scoring](../../scripts/eval-golden.ts#L239)

- **Completed draft, verification starved.** In `index.ts`, `run` awaits `agent.generate` and then `evaluateCitationGate` under the same signal. `citation-gate.ts` validates the labels and forwards the question, draft, account reads and the entire deduplicated admission set to `draft-verifier.ts`'s `createDraftVerifier`. Verification makes a second full `generateText` call to 6.1-sol at low effort using those inputs. It reuses retrieval and has no dedicated time reserve, output cap or check that enough time remains for a review and delivery. GS-018 and GS-053 drafted five times between them and lost all five to this path. GS-166 and three eventually scored items also hit it. GS-108 attempt 1 waited 27.269 s for the verifier after a 31.682 s draft. Block 5 GS-018 spent 39.973 s in verification after an 18.648 s draft. The verifier itself sometimes consumes a large share of the budget. [Gate](../../packages/agent-loop/src/citation-gate.ts#L39) · [Verifier](../../packages/agent-loop/src/draft-verifier.ts#L23) · [Attempt traces](kb-rerun22-diagnosis-evidence.md#full-run-attempt-traces)
- **Late progress and delivery lose authorization.** `TicketContextTokens.issue` in `packages/trusted-context/src/index.ts` expires the capsule 60 s after issuance, rounded to whole seconds. `run` starts its own later deadline and attempts to return a saved progress reply after expiration by calling `assertCurrent()`. The eval host's `assertCurrent` revalidates the same capsule. The fallback can therefore fail even with a saved reply, and authorization can expire just before the runner deadline even after a passing review. A mock-clock probe reproduced both cases. The timeout rows had empty replies and null gate outcomes. The harness preserves the provider timeout but drops the final exception name, so the exact outer error per live attempt is inferred from the code and replay. Authorization remains the delivery requirement. The defect is scheduling the last trusted check after its usable window. [Token expiry](../../packages/trusted-context/src/index.ts#L18) · [Fallback](../../packages/agent-loop/src/index.ts#L537) · [Deadline probes](kb-rerun22-diagnosis-evidence.md#deadline-and-cancellation-probes)
- **Draft call consumes the remaining budget.** `lunaAnswerOptions` in `model.ts` sets max effort and Fast. `run` calls non-streaming `agent.generate` with a 10-step cap but no per-call time or output-token cap. The step cap can only act between calls. In the full run, semantic prefetch put evidence into one prompt, disabled model tools and made one Luna call in every attempt. GS-023 and GS-030 each spent three attempts waiting for that call, with no draft to verify. Later tool-loop turns can carry earlier responses and tool outputs forward, but prompt growth across steps cannot explain these traces. Returned metadata reports max effort. There is no matched effort comparison or provider queue/inference split, so the duration attributable to max effort is unknown. [Luna middleware](../../packages/agent-loop/src/model.ts#L20) · [Prefetch and agent generation](../../packages/agent-loop/src/index.ts#L468)
- **Prefetch repeats work and has gaps in cancellation.** `search_support_knowledge` runs distinct focused searches in parallel with `Promise.all`. GS-030's four queries returned overlapping hits. The search tool memoizes requests within the attempt, and `SemanticKnowledgeProvider.vector` caches query embeddings across attempts in a shard. Four searches therefore need fewer than four new embedding requests once cached. `coRetrieveCompanions` reads the first 120 lines for every seed and awaits each read in order, even when several seeds point at the same article. Four of GS-030's eight reads per attempt repeat an article window. Companion expansion can also put repeated spans into the drafting input. The 33,260-byte GS-030 prompt and the repeated read work precede the call. Their contribution to model time is unmeasured. Independent required account reads and search are also awaited serially in prefetch. The regular tool loop sets `toolCallConcurrency: 1`, but that loop did no model tool work here. `SupportCoreClient.call` has a 10 s fetch timeout. The eval's injected fetch ignores its signal, and `SemanticKnowledgeProvider.similarity` and `OpenAIEmbeddings.embed` lack turn cancellation, with embeddings using a separate 30 s timeout. Slow retrieval can therefore delay or outlive the intended phase cutoff, even though the observed full-run timeouts happened after admissions. For grounding, `evaluateCitationGate` rejects any unadmitted label even when the cited interval is the union of two admitted spans and other valid labels are present. That is a reproducible draft-label construction failure for GS-062, both resumed GS-140 on drafts, and repeats of GS-023 and GS-094. Correct draft-judge scores establish coverage of the reference points and cannot settle whether every extra claim was supported. ## Fast and batch concurrency

All 40 Luna responses with settings show the SDK sent Fast and the provider returned `default`, with `max` effort in each response. Another 37 calls lack response settings. An intercepted SDK request confirms `service_tier: "fast"` leaves the client. The local sub2api source has a pass path that converts it to `priority` and a policy path that can remove it. Whether the deployed gateway sent `priority` upstream is unresolved. Correlate the live ingress, effective rule and selected upstream account, egress request tier and upstream response tier for the same request. An end-to-end response of `priority` or `fast` would confirm availability on that route. [Fast evidence](kb-rerun22-diagnosis-evidence.md#fast-and-concurrency)

The three processes ran concurrently and took **580.5, 546.9 and 552.9 s**. The slowest batch set the **580.6 s** wall time. Timeouts occupied **420.5 s** of that batch, from seven attempts that ran to the deadline. Each batch still waits serially for its own attempts, backoff and judging. Across all three batches, process time totaled 1,680.3 s and timeout attempts occupied 1,201.6 s. The resulting wall time is **43.4 s (7%)** below the approximate 624 s benchmark from the brief. That benchmark assumes 48 s per item, while this run carried 28 attempts for 13 items. With no identical serial run or upstream queue telemetry, the 7% is a benchmark comparison. No recorded 429s forced serial fallback. Keep bounded concurrency available for throughput, and use matched serial and three-way blocks before choosing it as the default for causal comparisons. ## Ranked fix targets and verification criteria

1. **Budget the full authorized delivery path.** Derive stage cutoffs from the earlier of the 60 s answer deadline and trusted capsule expiry. Reserve time for verification, final authorization and returning a supported reply or handoff. Bound the verifier's work, retain the finished draft and evidence within the attempt, and avoid starting speculative work or a full redraft when a bounded review can still finish. Fake-clock tests should cover a slow draft, a slow verifier, issuance near a second boundary and an outer cancellation. Require a typed stage outcome and a supported reply or handoff within budget while authorization is current. Canceled, expired or superseded turns must deny delivery. For R23, require zero timeout attempts and zero timeout-unscored rows in the clean group and scheduled durability samples.
2. **Make grounded drafts deliverable.** Give generation stable IDs for individual admitted spans and render the verified labels in code. Report the exact failed claim or reference, and make the independent policy routing check accept clear supported wording such as the saved GS-140 phrasing. Replay the combined ranges from GS-062, GS-140, GS-023 and GS-094. Require valid label construction and a live verifier pass for supported paraphrases. Keep all five gate rejection controls, GS-062's scoped route control and the explicit installer-state handoff. On trigger-on GS-140, require both policy articles and five supported, correct deliveries with explicit wait advice.
3. **Bound the first Luna request and remove redundant prefetch.** Deduplicate companion reads by article window and passages by label before drafting. Run independent Core reads with bounded concurrency and carry the phase signal through the eval transport and embedding work. Cap the first call's time and output with measured headroom for a complete max-effort answer and review. Reproduce GS-030 with at most one read per distinct article window, preserved route and DNS evidence, a smaller prompt and delivered coverage of automatic switching and faster DNS updates. Exercise a stalled read and embedding with a fake clock, and require the calls to settle within the phase cutoff. Report reasoning and output usage and compare first-call completion for GS-018, GS-023 and GS-030 with the same corpus and model.
4. **Verify the Fast route and measure batch throughput.** For a pinned Luna max request, record the ingress, policy decision, account route, upstream tier and response tier. Count Fast only on a returned `fast` or `priority`. Run matched serial and three-way blocks with the same corpus, model, verifier, retry policy and observed tier. Record per-item outcomes, deadline hits, first-attempt delivery and total wall time. Keep three-way concurrency for comparable results when it consistently reduces wall time with no increase in timeouts, 429s or failed deliveries.
5. **Re-establish content durability.** Repeat GS-018, GS-023, GS-062 and GS-094 in five scheduled clean samples each. Require the upload connection check before compatibility and a cited SFTP fallback for GS-018. Require cache flush, Google DNS and explicit conditional rejoin for GS-023, scoped routing and no-speedup advice for GS-062, and version before reinstall across applicable loaders for GS-094. Require five of five correct deliveries per item with valid citations. Track both gate and judge outcomes for GS-084, GS-108 and GS-163. Keep clean/fix-4 GS-056 as the paired corpus control and use the fixed GS-023 drafts to detect judge drift under the same rubric.
6. **Verify answers against cleared policy.** Until the owner rules, require documented cancellation, renewal and backup steps where applicable, with disputed timers and case-specific eligibility deferred. Check GS-166 for actionable SFTP connection and drag-and-drop guidance. After the owner confirms thresholds, destinations and retention rules, align the KB and the provisional scoring reference, rescore the saved drafts and require five supported fresh deliveries per affected item against the approved policy.
